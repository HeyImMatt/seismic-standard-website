Frequently Asked Questions

What genre do you play?

We play retro-inspired analog rock with influences from classic rock, indie, and alternative. Our sound is warm, energetic, and perfect for venues looking for something with both nostalgia and edge.

How far do you travel for shows?

We're based in Portland and regularly play throughout the metro area. For shows outside the area, we're happy to discuss travel arrangements on a case-by-case basis.

What's your typical rate?

Our rates vary depending on the venue, event type, set length, and other factors. Please contact us for a quote tailored to your specific needs.

How much space do you need?

We're a 4-piece band with a modest setup. We typically need a stage area of at least 12x16 feet, but we can adapt to smaller spaces if needed.
