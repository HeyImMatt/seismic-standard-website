About

Seismic Standard brings an infectious blend of folk warmth and indie rock spirit to Portland's music scene. Their sound is anchored by lush harmonies, a mix of acoustic and electric tones and the rattle of an old-timey washboard outfitted with all the bells and whistles.

The trio of Shawn Henderson, Matt McFarland and Giued Hatch craft original songs and creative covers that mosey easily between laidback grooves and rowdy, foot-stomping fun.

Seismic Standard's songwriting blends the sounds of modern folk heroes like Fruitbats, Lumnieers, and M. Ward, with some nostalgic 90s alt-rock edge for a result that is catchy and instantly familiar.
